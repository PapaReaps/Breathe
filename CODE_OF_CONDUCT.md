# Code of conduct

Breathe is for people who find everyday life hard, and it's built by someone who does too. Everyone who joins in, whether reporting a problem, sharing an idea or writing code, helps keep it a calm and kind place.

## We ask everyone to

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here have ADHD, anxiety, health conditions or a lot on their plate. Assume good intentions, and give people time.
- **Use plain, gentle words.** No jargon to make someone feel small, and no shouting, mocking or pressure.
- **Respect privacy.** Never share someone's personal details, and never post real money numbers, passwords, PINs or passphrases, yours or anyone else's. Made-up examples are perfect.
- **Welcome everyone,** whatever their background, age, disability, gender, sexuality, religion or experience. Beginners especially.
- **Disagree gently.** It's fine to see things differently. Talk about the idea, not the person.
- **Keep the founding promise in mind:** Breathe will never be about making money. It will always be about helping people.

## Not okay

- Insults, harassment, threats, or picking on someone.
- Sexual language or images.
- Sharing someone's private information without their clear permission.
- Spam, adverts, or using Breathe to sell something.
- Anything that makes someone feel ashamed for finding things hard.

## If something goes wrong

If someone is unkind, or you feel uncomfortable, please tell the person who looks after Breathe. On GitHub, use the **[Security tab](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/security)** → **Report a vulnerability** to send a private message. Everything is kept private and treated with care.

Depending on what happened, they may ask someone to stop, remove what was posted, or, for serious or repeated harm, block that person from the project.

## Where this applies

Everywhere people take part in Breathe: reports and ideas on GitHub, code changes, and anywhere someone speaks on Breathe's behalf.

Thank you for helping Breathe stay a calm, kind place for everyone.

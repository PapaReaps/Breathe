# Accessibility

Breathe Money is made for people who find everyday organisation hard, including people with ADHD, dyslexia, anxiety, low vision and health conditions that make long screens tiring. It should be easy to use on a tired, overwhelmed day.

## What it does

**Easy to read**
- **Text size** (Standard, Larger or Largest), on top of your phone or browser's own text size. Everything grows together, buttons included.
- **Higher contrast**: darker writing and stronger outlines, in light and dark mode. "Match my device" switches it on by itself if your device asks for it.
- **Light or dark**, or follow your device.
- **Nine app colours and nine savings colours**, each tested to stay easy to read in light and dark (an automatic check runs on every change).
- **Calm, plain wording**, with no jargon, shame or pressure. An automatic check stops words like "overdue", "failed" or "you must".

**Less to take in**
- **One question at a time** in the step-by-step setup, and every question can be skipped.
- **A quiet home screen**: you can hide everything except your number.
- **Short pages**: App options is a list, and each part opens on its own.
- **Movement is switched off** for people who ask for reduced motion.

**Works with**
- **The keyboard alone**: Tab to move, Enter to choose, Escape to close the menu. A clear focus ring shows where you are, thicker in higher contrast.
- **Screen readers**: every box, choice and button has a label; hints and gentle problems are linked to their box; new messages are announced.
- **Feeling a tap** instead of hearing it: optional vibration (where the phone allows it) and optional sounds, both off unless chosen.

## Known limits, honestly

- **Tested with TalkBack on Android** on a real phone, over several days, including by someone with very low vision: no problems found. **VoiceOver on iPhone and NVDA or Narrator on Windows are not yet tested by hand.** It's built for them, but those real-device checks are still to do.
- **Not yet checked at 200% browser zoom combined with Largest text.**
- **Vibration** isn't available on iPhones or computers, because they don't allow websites to vibrate.
- **Sounds** play at the phone's media volume, and may still play when the phone is on silent. They're off unless you choose one.

The full checklist is in [docs/STAGE-9.md](docs/STAGE-9.md#accessibility-checklist).

## If something gets in your way

If anything is hard to read, hard to reach, or doesn't work with the tools you use, please tell us. In the app: **Menu → Settings → Help and sharing → Report a problem**. Or open a report on [GitHub](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/issues/new/choose). We'll treat it as a real problem, because it is.

**A smoother switch.**

## What's new

- **Switching parts is smoother.** After you pick Money or Medication, the screen now fades back in straight onto the part you picked, instead of showing the old one for a moment first.

Nothing changes with your numbers or medicines, and all your choices stay as they were.

## Updating to this version

- **Android:** you'll see an **Update ready** pop-up, or tap the **gear → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers and medicines stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice. Breathe Medication is a reminder tool, not medical advice.

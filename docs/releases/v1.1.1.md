**Breathe has a new look, and one name.**

## What's new

- **One name: Breathe.** The app holds Breathe Money and Breathe Medication now, so the whole app is simply called **Breathe**: in your phone's settings, the browser tab and the share page. Inside, Breathe Money and Breathe Medication keep their own names and colours.
- **The Breathe family logo.** The app icon, and the little logo at the top, are now the coloured rings: lavender, blue and mint around a calm white centre. It works on round-icon phones and with Android's themed icons too.
- **Medication's pages have Back.** In Breathe Medication's menu, every page now has **← Back to the menu** and **Done** at the top, just like Breathe Money.

Nothing changes with your numbers or medicines, and the download link stays the same.

## Updating to this version

- **Android:** you'll see an **Update ready** pop-up, or go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers and medicines stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself. A home-screen icon you added before may keep the old picture until you add it again.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice. Breathe Medication is a reminder tool, not medical advice.

**Small tidy-ups.**

## What's new

- **The name under the icon is now just "Breathe"**, so your home screen shows it in full instead of "Breathe …". The app is still called Breathe Money everywhere else.
- **Report a problem** now opens two simple forms: **Something isn't working** and **An idea to make it better**. Each asks a few gentle questions, and reminds you never to include your real numbers. Safety problems can be reported privately instead.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay. The new name may take a moment to show, or appear after restarting the phone.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** download `breathe-money-v0.18.1.apk` below and open it on your phone. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

**A little simpler, all round.**

## What's new

- **A lighter first question in setup.** It now just asks when your money comes in and how often. What happens on a weekend or bank holiday is one short line, set to "the working day before", with **Change** if yours is different. "Any other money coming in?" is now its own question.
- **Your data and backups is clearer.** It says "Your numbers are saved on this device" when they are (it used to say "Nothing to save yet", which sounded worrying). The backup buttons are near the top, and the "Good to know" notes are lower down.
- **A shorter Menu.** Bank holidays moved into **Settings**, since you set it once and leave it.
- **Your account** shows "Checked today." The date box only appears if you tap **Checked it on another day?**, or if your balance is from an earlier day.
- **Where this number comes from** shows your bills as one line, **Bills (4)**. Tap it to see each one.
- The line under your number is shorter: *Money comes in Fri 27 Nov · 21 days*.

Nothing about how your number is worked out has changed.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

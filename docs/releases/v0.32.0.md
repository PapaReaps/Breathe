**A little life in the opening breath.**

## What's new

- When you open Breathe Money, a few **tiny dots now drift gently upward** around "breathe" while it takes its breath, like air bubbles. They're in the same colour as the word.
- **Same speed as before:** the breath and the float up to your number take exactly as long as they did. The dots start during the breath and fade away during the float. Tapping still skips straight to your number.
- They stay off if your phone is set to **reduce motion**, or if you've switched off **Let "breathe" breathe** in Settings → Home screen.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

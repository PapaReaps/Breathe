**A calmer, shorter menu, and Settings behind a gear.**

## What's new

- **A settings gear at the top.** Tap the round gear next to **Menu** to open **Settings**. It's the same page in Breathe Money and Breathe Medication, and holds everything about Breathe itself: Look and colours, Text and contrast, Sounds and vibration, Privacy screen, Updates, Your data and backups, Help and sharing, and About Breathe.
- **Each Menu now only holds its own things.**
  - **Breathe Money:** Your account, Your money, Calendar, and **Money options** (currency, home screen, balance reminder, step-by-step setup and bank holidays).
  - **Breathe Medication:** your medicines, History and Reminders.
- **One About Breathe page** for the whole app.
- **Back still goes back one step**, from the button at the top or your phone's own Back.

Nothing changes with your numbers or medicines, and all your choices stay as they were.

## Updating to this version

- **Android:** you'll see an **Update ready** pop-up, or tap the **gear → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers and medicines stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice. Breathe Medication is a reminder tool, not medical advice.

## What's new (Android app)

- **A clear update button.** When a new version is out, an **Update ready · Get it** button now shows under your number. One tap opens Updates, where **Download and install** is.
- **Asked once.** The first time you open the app after this update, a small note at the bottom asks: **Hear about new versions?** Tap **Yes, check for me** and the app checks GitHub once a day when you open it. Tap **Not now** and it won't ask again. Nothing about you or your numbers is ever sent.
- You can still change this any time in **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**.

The web link and computer app update by themselves, so they don't ask.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

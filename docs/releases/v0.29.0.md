**Tidier lists, jet black, and lots more colour.**

## What's new

- **Tidier lists.** Money coming in and Bills now show just what it is and how much, with **in** or **out** after the amount. Tap one to see or change its date.
- **Jet black.** A new choice in **Look and colours → Light or dark**: a true black background, easy on the eyes at night.
- **22 app colours**, including six deeper ones: Deep teal, Navy, Deep purple, Burgundy, Forest and Deep orange. Plus Indigo, Coral, Sage, Lavender, Sky, Berry and Gold. Every one is checked to stay easy to read.
- **Simple lists for choices.** App colour, savings colour, sounds and vibration are now drop-down lists, with **Hear it** and **Feel it** buttons to try a sound or buzz first.
- **Box outlines.** Choose the colour of the lines around boxes: soft grey, your app colour, white, gold, rose, sky, or none.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

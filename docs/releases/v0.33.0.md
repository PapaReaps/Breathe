**Simpler Menu, gold main money, and colours you can see.**

## What's new

- **Your money, all on one page.** The Menu is now just **Your account**, **Your money**, **Calendar** and **Settings**. Your money has money coming in, bills, food and travel, and savings together.
- **"Where this number comes from" has gone** from the Menu. Every amount now shows **in** or **out** right where it is, so you can see how it works at a glance.
- **Your main money is gold** in the Calendar: a gold dot on its day, and its amount in gold in the list.
- **Colours you can see.** In Look and colours, app colours, savings colours and box outlines are now circles in their real colour, with the name underneath. Tap one to choose it.
- **12 deeper savings colours**: Deep gold, Ruby, Emerald, Sapphire, Amethyst, Copper, Jade, Bronze, Graphite, Crimson, Ocean deep and Orchid. That's 21 in all.
- **Breathe apps.** Tap the little logo and **Breathe Money ▾** at the top to see the Breathe family. Breathe Money is the home for them all, and new apps will open from there when they're ready.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

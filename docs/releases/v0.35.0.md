**Breathe Money now works in 20 currencies.**

## What's new

- **Choose your currency.** Pound, euro, US dollar, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand dollars, Swiss franc, Swedish krona, Norwegian and Danish krone, Polish złoty, Czech koruna, Indian rupee, South African rand, Singapore and Hong Kong dollars, Mexican peso, Brazilian real, Philippine peso and Nigerian naira.
- **Setup asks first**, with a guess from your phone's language and region. Change it any time in **Menu → Settings → Currency**.
- **Amounts are written your country's way**, like 1.234,56 € on a German phone. You can type amounts either way too (12.50 or 12,50).
- **Only the symbol changes.** Nothing is converted, nothing is looked up online, and your numbers stay exactly the same.
- **Already using Breathe Money? Nothing changes:** you stay on the pound unless you pick another one.
- **Kinder words for everyone, not only the UK:** examples now say "wages, benefits or a pension". Outside the UK, Bank holidays says to pick the first choice and add your own public holidays if you like.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** you'll see an **Update ready** pop-up (from v0.34.0), or go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

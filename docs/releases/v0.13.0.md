**Built around the money you have coming in, and a better fit on Android.**

## What's new

- **Money coming in, not payday.** Breathe Money no longer assumes you have a payday. The first question is now **"When does your main money next come in?"**. That might be wages, Universal Credit, a pension or PIP. Your number lasts until that day: **"Safe to spend until your money comes in."** Any other money due before then (like a refund) goes on the same page.
- **Savings.** The rainy-day pot is now called **Savings**, and the word Savings in the menu shows in your savings colour.
- **A tidier menu.** Step-by-step setup, App options (the old Settings page), Your data and backups, and About now sit together inside **Settings**.
- **Share Breathe Money.** In **Menu → Settings**, copy or share the website link or the Android download page to send to someone.
- **Report a problem.** In **Menu → Settings**, this opens a GitHub page to tell us what happened (you'll need a free GitHub account). Nothing is sent from the app.

## Fixes

- **Android: the app fits the screen.** The top and bottom no longer go under the phone's status bar and buttons. A soft shaded strip sits behind each one. The status bar icons follow your light or dark choice.
- **Android: menus stop where they should.** Some pages, like Settings, could be scrolled right off the screen. Now only the middle part scrolls.

## Updating to this version

- **Android:** download `breathe-money-v0.13.0.apk` below and install it over the old one. Your numbers stay, including your saved payday date, which is now your "money comes in" date.
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

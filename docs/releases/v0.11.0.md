**A new way to open: one deep breath.**

## What's new

- **The opening is now a deep breath.** The word "breathe" starts light, fills out and rises a little as it breathes in, holds, then settles, with a soft shadow beneath it, like a chest rising and falling. It takes about three seconds, then the app fades in.
- It's the same in the Android app and on the web link, and follows your light or dark setting.
- **If your phone is set to reduce motion**, it shows the still word instead, which fades as soon as the app is ready.
- It only plays when the app starts fresh, not every time you switch back to it.
- The new rounded font (Nunito, free to use) is built into the app, so the opening still works offline and nothing is fetched from anywhere.

## Updating

- **Android:** download `breathe-money-v0.11.0.apk` below and install it over the old one. Your numbers stay.
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself. Close and reopen it once.

Everything else is the same as [v0.10.0](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/tag/v0.10.0). Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

**Savings, kept separate.**

People told us they'd like savings to sit on their own, so that's how they work now.

## What's new

- **Savings never change your number.** Type in what you've saved, and use **+ Add** or **− Take out** whenever it changes. Nothing else moves: not your number, and not your balance.
- **If you had savings set before**, your safe-to-spend number will **go up by that amount** after updating, because it's no longer taken off. That's expected. Your savings are still shown, just separately.
- The home screen badge now simply says **Savings £…**.
- Setup asks *Do you have any savings?*, just for keeping track.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

**Calmer Settings.**

## What's new

- **App options is now a short list.** Look and colours, Text and contrast, Home screen, Balance reminder, Sounds and vibration, Privacy screen and Updates. Each shows your current choice, and opens its own small page with just those choices. Every feature is still there.
- **A shorter Settings folder.** Four items instead of six: Step-by-step setup, App options, Your data and backups, and **Help and sharing**, which holds Share Breathe Money, Report a problem and About.
- **The update note on the home screen** now opens the Updates page directly.

## Trying it out

This is the version for trying out with family and friends. If something doesn't work, or there's an idea to make it better, go to **Menu → Settings → Help and sharing → Report a problem**.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** download `breathe-money-v0.18.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

**Simpler, with less to read.**

## What's new

- **Settings is now one page.** Look and colours, Text and contrast, Home screen, Sounds, Balance reminder, Privacy screen and Updates are all on it, in three small groups, with your setup, bank holidays and backups underneath. Colours are now 2 taps away instead of 3.
- **Your account is just the question and the box.** Typing your balance always means "this is what's in my account now".
- **Shorter titles:** "Bills before your money comes in" is now just **Bills**, with a one-line hint. Money coming in is shorter too.
- **Plan until is tucked away.** Money coming in shows your payments and one line saying which one your number plans until. Tap **More options** to change it.
- **A calmer Savings page:** your total, then **+ Add** and **− Take out**. Undo only appears right after a change.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates** (from this version: **Menu → Settings → Updates**), tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

**Share Breathe with a QR code.**

## What's new

- **Share Breathe Money now shows a QR code.** A friend points their phone camera at your screen, and Breathe starts downloading. No scrolling, no searching. Find it in **Menu → Settings → Help and sharing → Share Breathe Money**.
- **A switch for your friend's phone:** **Android** gives the app, and **iPhone or computer** gives the web version, which works in any browser.
- **Copy link** and **Share** send the same link, so you can text it instead.
- **A one-tap download link** that always gets the newest Android app: [breathe-money.apk](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk). The QR code is made on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

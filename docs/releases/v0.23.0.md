**A warmer welcome, and a little more privacy.**

Thank you again to the testers.

## What's new

- **A calm opening.** "breathe" takes its deep breath, then slowly floats up the screen while it keeps breathing, and settles above your number. On a first visit, the welcome fades in underneath: *Take a breath. You've got this.* Tap anywhere to skip.
- **"breathe" stays above your number**, breathing slowly (one breath every 6 seconds). Prefer it still? **Menu → Settings → App options → Home screen → Let "breathe" breathe.** It's always still if your phone is set to reduce motion.
- **Your money's name stays private.** The home screen just says *Money comes in Fri 27 Nov · 21 days*. If you'd like the name shown, switch on **Show what your money is called** in the same place. It still shows inside the Menu and Coming up.
- **Setup is a little simpler.** Question 2 shows your main money at the top, so it never seems to disappear, and no longer asks "How sure are you?". Extra money added in setup counts as sure. You can still mark something as "might not arrive" in **Menu → Money coming in**.

Nothing about how your number is worked out has changed.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

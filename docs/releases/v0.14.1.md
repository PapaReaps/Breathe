## What's new

- **Show my savings under the number.** A new switch in **Menu → Settings → App options → Home screen**. Turn it off, together with the short sentence and the date, and the home screen shows just your number. Your savings are still kept aside: this only hides the reminder.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from Breathe Money. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions):** download `breathe-money-v0.14.1.apk` below and install it over the old one.
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

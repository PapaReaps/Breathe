**Give your main money a name.**

Thank you to the testers who spotted this.

## What's new

- **Question 1 of setup now starts with "What is it?"**, like every other kind of money. Type Universal Credit, Wages, Pension, or whatever you call it. It's optional, so you can leave it empty. (Before, the first box was the date, so tapping it opened the calendar.)
- **Your name for it shows around the app:** under your number (*Universal Credit comes in Fri 27 Nov · 21 days*), in the **Menu**, and in **Coming up**.
- You can add or change it any time in **Menu → Money coming in**.

Nothing about how your number is worked out has changed, and your saved numbers stay as they are.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

**Small safety fixes, and tidier words.**

## Fixes

- **The PIN pause stays.** After several wrong PINs, the app asks you to wait a moment. Closing and opening the app again no longer skips that wait. Typing the right PIN afterwards clears it as usual.
- **Tidier storage.** After **Download and install**, the downloaded update file is removed the next time you open the app.
- **Savings, everywhere.** The last mention of the old "rainy-day pot" is now "savings".

These came from a safety check of the whole app, which found nothing serious.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions):** download `breathe-money-v0.17.1.apk` below and install it over the old one.
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

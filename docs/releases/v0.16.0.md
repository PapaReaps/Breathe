## What's new

- **Sound when I tap.** In **Menu → Settings → App options**, choose one of twelve soft sounds: Soft tap, Classic click, Wooden block, Pebble, Bubble, Water drop, Soft pop, Marimba, Gentle chime, Little breath, Clock tick or Heartbeat. You hear each one as you choose it. The sounds are made inside the app, so nothing is downloaded, and they play at your phone's media volume.
- **Vibrate when I tap.** Gentle, Medium or Firm, using your phone's own vibration. This uses one new Android permission, "vibrate", which can do nothing else.
- **Both start Off.** Nothing changes unless you choose it.
- **Which version you have.** **App options → Updates** now says "You have version 0.16.0", so it's easy to check.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions):** download `breathe-money-v0.16.0.apk` below and install it over the old one.
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

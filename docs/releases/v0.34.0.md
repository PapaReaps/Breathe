**Update ready: a pop-up when there's a new version.**

## What's new

- **Update ready pop-up (Android).** When a new version of Breathe Money is out, a calm pop-up shows in the middle of the home screen. Tap **Update now** and it downloads and starts installing straight away. Android will then ask if you want to update: tap **Update**. Your numbers stay.
- **Later** hides the pop-up until the next version. The small **Update ready** button under your number stays, so you can still get it any time.
- It only shows if you said yes to **Hear about new versions?** (or turned on checking in **Menu → Settings → Updates**). Breathe checks GitHub at most once a day, and nothing about you or your numbers is sent.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay. From now on, the pop-up will tell you.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

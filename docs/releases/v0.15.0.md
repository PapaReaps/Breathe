**A new icon, more colours, and easier updates.**

## What's new

- **A new app icon, "Breathing out".** Soft ripples on the app's teal, on your home screen, in the browser tab and in your bookmarks. On phones with themed icons, it takes on your wallpaper colours.
- **Eight more colours.** In **Menu → Settings → App options**: four more app colours (Ocean, Terracotta, Slate and Amber) and four more savings colours (Peach, Mint, Sand and Silver). Every colour is checked to stay easy to read in light and dark.

## Fixes

- **The "Get it" note on the home screen** now opens **App options → Updates**, where **Download and install** is, instead of the GitHub page.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions):** download `breathe-money-v0.15.0.apk` below and install it over the old one.
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

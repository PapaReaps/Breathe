**Only what's really there.**

## What's new

- **Your number never counts money that isn't here yet.** Money coming in before your main money (like Child Benefit) now only counts from the day it arrives. Before, it was added straight away, so the number could be more than you actually had.
- **A small badge says when more is coming**, under your number: for example **+£140.00 on Mon 9 Nov**. On that day, your number goes up by itself.
- Bills due before that money arrives are taken off first, so what you see is safe to spend **right now**, and it still leaves enough for everything until your money comes in.
- **Where this number comes from** explains how much is waiting for the money still to come.
- If bills are due before the money arrives and there isn't enough, it says so kindly: "Things are tight until Mon 9 Nov".

Nothing to set up: it uses the dates you've already added.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

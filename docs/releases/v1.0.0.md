**Breathe Money 1.0 is ready.**

Breathe Money answers one question, calmly: *how much can I spend until my money comes in?*

Version 1.0 means it's ready for anyone to rely on. It was built one small stage at a time, used every day by family and friends, tested with TalkBack (the Android screen reader), and checked for safety. Thank you to everyone who tried it early and shared ideas: so much of it came from you.

## What's new

- **A short "Breathe Money is ready" note**, shown once to everyone who was already using it. Your numbers are just as you left them.

## What you get

- **One calm number:** safe to spend until your money comes in, after your bills, food and travel.
- **Everything in one place:** money coming in, bills, food and travel, and savings on one Your money page, each with its own **in** or **out**.
- **A Calendar** as a simple list or a month, with your main money in gold.
- **20 currencies**, written your country's way.
- **22 app colours and 21 savings colours**, light, dark or jet black, bigger text and higher contrast.
- **Private by design:** no accounts, no adverts, nothing sent anywhere. Your numbers stay on your device, with optional passphrase-protected backups and a PIN privacy screen.
- **Updates in the Android app** with an **Update ready** pop-up.
- **Free, always.**

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** you'll see an **Update ready** pop-up, or go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

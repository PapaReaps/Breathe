**Breathe Money: how much can I spend until payday?** One calm number, after your bills, food, travel and a rainy-day pot. Free, private and kind.

This is the first release, and an early test version. It works, but it's still being built one small stage at a time.

## Two ways to use it

- **Android:** download `breathe-money-v0.10.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Android will ask whether to allow "install unknown apps" for this one install, because it isn't from the Play Store. [Step-by-step help](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md#installing-the-apk).
- **Any phone or computer, including iPhone:** open **[papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/)**, then add it to your home screen.

## What it does

- **Your safe-to-spend number**, until payday, with every pound explained line by line.
- **Gentle step-by-step setup**: one question at a time, and every question can be skipped.
- **Paydays and bills that repeat**: weekly, every 2 or 4 weeks, or monthly.
- **UK bank holidays built in** (England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland).
- **Light or dark mode**, five app colours, five rainy-day pot colours, and a quiet home screen if you want one.
- **Text size and higher contrast**, keyboard-friendly, and built for screen readers.
- **An optional PIN privacy screen.**
- **Backup files protected with a passphrase** (encrypted), or plain.
- **Works offline.** A calm one-breath opening, under a second.

## Your numbers stay yours

No accounts, no tracking, no adverts. Your numbers are kept only on your phone, and are never sent anywhere. The Android app also switches off Android's Google Drive backup, so nothing is copied off the phone. [Privacy, in plain English](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

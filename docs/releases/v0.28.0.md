**No more "main money".**

## What's new

- **Just add all your money coming in.** Wages, Universal Credit, PIP, Child Benefit, a pension: each one with how much and how often. There's no "main" one to choose any more.
- **Your number plans until your biggest regular payment, by itself.** If you plan around a different one, pick it in **Menu → Money coming in → Plan until**.
- Every other payment counts from the day it arrives, with the small **+£** badge, which now sits just above the "Money comes in" line, because it comes first.
- **Setup is one question shorter:** it starts with "What money comes in?"

**Already using Breathe Money?** Nothing changes for you: your main money is now simply in your Money coming in list, and your number stays exactly the same. You can add its amount any time, but you don't have to.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

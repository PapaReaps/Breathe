**Breathe Medication is here, inside Breathe.**

## What's new

- **Breathe Medication.** Tap **Breathe Money ▾** at the top, then **Add** next to Breathe Medication. Nothing changes in Breathe Money unless you add it, and Breathe opens on whichever you used last. Medication has its own sage green, so you always know where you are.
- **A calm list for today**, grouped into morning, afternoon and evening. Tap **Taken** when it's done (tap again to undo). Nothing is ever red or "missed".
- **Gentle reminders (Android app).** Breathe asks once if you'd like them, then Android asks for permission. One note per time, with **Taken** and **Later**, and one nudge 30 minutes later if it isn't ticked. Then it stays quiet. Reminders never show medicine names. If your phone holds them back to save battery, a calm note shows the setting to change.
- **Running low.** Count what's left, and get a soft note about a week before it runs out. **I've ordered it** rests the note until you add more.
- **History.** A month of quiet dots: all taken, some, or none logged. Tap a day to see what was due. No streaks, no scores.
- **Shared, and private.** Look and colours, text size and the PIN privacy screen work across both. Backup files now include your medicines, and clearing your data removes them too. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Breathe Medication is a reminder tool, not medical advice. Questions about your medicines? Check with your pharmacist or GP.

## Updating to this version

- **Android:** you'll see an **Update ready** pop-up, or go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself. On the web, reminders can't pop up: keep the list handy and check it.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

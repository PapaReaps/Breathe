**A tidier switcher and menu.**

## What's new

- **Breathe apps is one simple list again.** Tap the name at the top to see Breathe Money and Breathe Medication (**You're here**, **Open** or **Add**), with the apps still to come underneath. No extra pictures or paragraphs.
- **Breathe Medication's menu is tidier.** **Your data and backups** sits on its own line, just like in Breathe Money.

Nothing changes with your numbers or medicines.

## Updating to this version

- **Android:** you'll see an **Update ready** pop-up, or go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers and medicines stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice. Breathe Medication is a reminder tool, not medical advice.

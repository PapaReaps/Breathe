**Tidier taps and a neater Settings list.**

## What's new

- **No more coloured flash when you tap.** On Android, tapping **Breathe Money ▾**, **Menu** or any other button showed a coloured rectangle for a moment. That's gone everywhere in the app. Buttons now fade very slightly while you press them instead. Keyboard users still see a clear outline.
- **Every Settings row lines up**, even with bigger phone text. The grey words on the right are shorter so they fit on one line: **Balance reminder** shows **Off**, **Daily** or **Weekly**, and **Look and colours** shows **Auto** when it follows your phone. The choices themselves haven't changed.

## Updating to this version

- **Android:** you'll see an **Update ready** pop-up, or go to **Menu → Settings → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions, or a first install):** on your phone, tap [this download link](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/releases/latest/download/breathe-money.apk), then open it. [How to install it](https://github.com/PapaReaps/Breathe/blob/main/docs/ANDROID-APP.md).
- **Web link and computer app:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

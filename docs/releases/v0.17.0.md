## What's new

- **Coming up.** A new page in the **Menu**. It shows the days that matter from today until your money comes in: each bill with its date and amount, any money you're sure is coming, and the day your money comes in. Underneath, it adds up the bills before then.
- **List or Month.** It starts as a simple list. Tap **Month** at the top to see a calendar page instead: bill days in soft pink, money day in your app colour, and a ring round today. Tap a day to see what's on it. The app remembers which you chose.
- **Only what's sure.** Money that might come isn't shown. When it arrives, add it under **Money coming in**.

## Updating to this version

- **Android (from v0.14.0):** go to **Menu → Settings → App options → Updates**, tap **Check now**, then **Download and install**. Your numbers stay.
- **Android (older versions):** download `breathe-money-v0.17.0.apk` below and install it over the old one.
- **Web link:** [papareaps.github.io/Breathe](https://papareaps.github.io/Breathe/) updates by itself, as always.

Breathe Money is an organisation tool, not financial advice.

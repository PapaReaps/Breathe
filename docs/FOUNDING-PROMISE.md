# Our founding promise

> **Breathe will never be about making money. It will always be about helping people.**

## What this means in practice

- Breathe is free and open source.
- Essential features will always be free.
- No adverts. No selling or sharing of data. We do not hold data to sell.
- Nothing is designed to keep you hooked. No streaks, pressure or guilt.
- Manual use must always be possible. You are never forced to connect a bank or create an account.
- If an optional paid service ever exists (for example, a bank connection that creates real running costs), it may only cover those costs. It can never take away or weaken a free feature.

## How we use it

Every plan, feature and design choice is checked against this promise. The checklist is in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and significant decisions are recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` with a note on how they fit the promise.
